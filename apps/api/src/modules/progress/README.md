# progress module

Enrolments, how far each learner has got, and the aggregates the learner pages read.

| Aspect       | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Routes       | `GET /me/progress`, `GET /me/dashboard`, `GET /me/activity`, `GET /me/consistency`, `GET /me/deadlines`, `GET /students/:id/progress`, `GET /students/:id/consistency`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Permissions  | `progress:read` (scope `all`, `batch` or `own`); the dashboard is available to any signed-in learner                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Enrolments   | Derived, never entered by hand. `EnrollmentService` is the only writer: publishing a course enrols the audience, unpublishing removes the rows with no progress, and a student joining a batch picks up what that batch already has (`batch.members_changed`). A learner who leaves every batch a publication reaches becomes `withdrawn`: access ends, the record stays, and rejoining makes it active (or completed) again (Phase 2, D4; ADR-0010's own remedy)                                                                                                                                |
| Percentages  | One source of truth (Phase 2, D1). A course is completed mandatory lessons over mandatory lessons; a course whose author marked nothing mandatory counts every lesson, so the figure is never divided by zero. Every enrolment is recomputed when the outline changes. The overall figure is the mean of the course percentages (`overallPercent` in `@lms/shared`), as the staff reports average them                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Watched      | Measured by the server (Phase 2, D2; `watching.ts`): each report is credited with how far playback moved since the previous one, at most twice the time that really passed and at most 60 seconds, and a `watch` lesson completes on that measure, never on a figure the browser sends. There is one clock per learner: a report counts only the time since their last report on any lesson, and their reports are taken one at a time (an advisory lock), so playing several videos at once earns no more than one. The percentage shown only ever grows, so scrubbing backwards undoes nothing |
| Completion   | Recorded once, even when two callers complete the same lesson at the same moment (a lab lesson's own submission and the listener below): only the call that changes it to `completed` writes the activity row and emits `lesson.completed`; the last mandatory lesson also completes the enrolment and emits `course.completed`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Dashboard    | The institute's day (today and tomorrow, classes not yet over), what to carry on with, the overall figures, live announcements, the latest notifications, recent activity, the Labs and the certificate count, in one request; an error is shown as an error, never as "nothing to do"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Deadlines    | `GET /me/deadlines`: the learner's assignment due dates and test windows closing over a range of the institute's days, each marked done once handed in or sat; the schedule's week view shows them                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Activity     | `ActivityListener` records assignments answered (once per assignment per day), tests submitted by the learner (not auto-submitted) and labs solved for the first time, beside the lessons and courses `ProgressService` records                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Pending      | Assignments, tests and attendance are named with the reason they cannot be measured yet, rather than shown as zero (plan DP-A01)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Access       | `LearnerAccessService` answers "may this caller see this learner": their own record always, anyone within `all` scope, and only shared batches within `batch` scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Lab lessons  | `LabLessonsListener` hears `execution.completed` and, for a passed, trusted practice submission, completes every lab lesson that opens that lab in a course the learner is enrolled in — one in a module not yet unlocked included, since the work is done                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Events       | Publishes `course.completed`; subscribes to `batch.members_changed` (joins and removals), `execution.completed`, `submission.created` and `attempt.submitted`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Dependencies | Reads course, lesson and class tables through Prisma rather than importing those modules, so `courses` can depend on this module without a cycle. The dashboard uses the schedule module's pure row mapper                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Tests        | `test/courses.e2e-spec.ts` (recording, recomputation, the staff view, consistency between the progress page and the dashboard); `test/labs.e2e-spec.ts` (lab lessons completed in the lesson, from the catalogue and on opening, once each); `test/learner.e2e-spec.ts` (watch credit, completion rules, withdrawal and return, deadlines, the dashboard); `watching.spec.ts` (invariants over 2,000 generated reports)                                                                                                                                                                          |

Progress is only ever written through this module. Anything that shows a percentage asks for it here,
which is why the player, the course page, the dashboard and the staff view cannot disagree.

## Phases 3 and 4 (3 October 2026)

- **Staff read a learner within their reach (D6).** `overview(studentId, userId, within)`: someone
  who teaches some of a learner's batches sees the courses and activity reached through those
  batches only.
- **The trainer's dashboard (TRN-01)** adds the courses they teach and author, the next deadlines,
  the institute's day (its time zone), and review counts that match the review queues
  (`reviewableWhere`, shared with the queue).
- **Tests:** `test/staff-scope.e2e-spec.ts`, `test/trainer-workflows.e2e-spec.ts`.

## Phase 5 (3 October 2026)

- **One lifecycle** (`EnrollmentService`, phase record §6): a course reaches a learner through a
  live publication while their account is active or invited and the batch and programme are not
  archived; a new learner is enrolled in it at once (`student.created`). An open course no longer
  reached is withdrawn (D4) and comes back if reached again; a completed course is never
  withdrawn (L4); a withdrawn course does not complete or certify.
  Archiving a batch or programme, deactivation and reactivation apply it inside the request.
- **`progress.reconcile`** (every 15 minutes, `reconciliation.service.ts`) re-derives places of
  deactivated accounts and every learner's enrolments from the records: idempotent, audited when
  it changes anything, and failing the job when a learner could not be settled. Tests:
  `test/lifecycle.e2e-spec.ts`, `test/reconciliation.e2e-spec.ts`.
